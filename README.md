# Sami Magdouli

Co-founder and CTO of [NTA](https://nachteilsausgleich.app), a learning app for students with dyslexia. Based in Berlin, coding since 2020, final year of school (Abitur 2027).

## What I build

**NTA** (private repo). Learning app for students with dyslexia, plus the website, a CRM for our school outreach and a booking flow. Next.js, Claude API, self hosted.

**Emil** (private repo). A personal assistant that runs my mail, calendar and parcel tracking over WhatsApp and Telegram: one orchestrator, eight specialist agents, Claude API. The scraping part is public as [scraping-agent-skeleton](https://github.com/sami-mag07/scraping-agent-skeleton).

**ParkIt**. What parking in Berlin actually costs right now, with a community layer for cheap and free spots. [Mobile app](https://github.com/sami-mag07/ParkIt) and [web version](https://github.com/sami-mag07/parkit-web).

**AI workshops for teachers**. I ran workshops at my school on working with AI. The [handouts](https://github.com/sami-mag07/ki-workshop-handouts) and the [fact checker](https://github.com/sami-mag07/ki-faktencheck) that verifies an AI answer claim by claim are open.

**Beacon** (private repo). Crisis cockpit for winter blackouts, built at the European Defense Tech Week hackathon in Berlin, July 2026.

## Stack

TypeScript, React, Next.js, Python, Claude API, Docker on my own VPS.
